# Health data consent, 2026-11-08

Online Consultation Booking v2, spec §6. The form now asks for current
symptoms and past illnesses or chronic conditions. The clinic is the only
named recipient. `{clinic}` is the clinic's name.

## en

```text
I consent to Find a Doc, Japan collecting the health information I enter in this form (current symptoms and past illnesses or chronic conditions) and providing it to {clinic} to arrange my consultation.
```

## ja

```text
本フォームに入力した健康に関する情報（現在の症状、既往歴・持病）を、診察の手配のためにFind a Doc, Japanが取得し、{clinic}に提供することに同意します。
```
