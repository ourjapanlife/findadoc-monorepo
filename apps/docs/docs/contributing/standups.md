# Standups

## Weekly Standup

Find a Doc holds regular standups where contributors can:

- Share progress on current tasks
- Ask questions and get help
- Coordinate with other contributors
- Learn about upcoming priorities

## How to Join

Join our [Slack workspace](https://find-a-doc.slack.com/join/shared_invite/zt-2lb3j2t1z-O9F4thzcFktqsIXNzzjzHw#/shared-invite/email) to get details on standup times and meeting links. Standup information is shared in the **#dev-team** channel.

## What to Expect

Standups are informal and welcoming. Whether you're a seasoned contributor or just getting started, you're encouraged to join and participate.
